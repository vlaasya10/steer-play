## Corrections made

- Changed “Carbohydrates are digested into absorbable sugars, including glucose.” to “Digestible carbohydrates are broken down into absorbable sugars, including glucose.” The earlier sentence overgeneralised digestion to all carbohydrates; terminology was also checked against [MedlinePlus dietary fibre](https://medlineplus.gov/dietaryfiber.html), which distinguishes fibre from carbohydrates broken down and absorbed.
- Added visible NHLBI and MedlinePlus citations for the heart and cell biology quiz answers; health content in interest rounds now displays its review status too.
- Added appetite/sleep context references to Missing Clue and the quiz result. Added prescribing/safety sources to the neutral metabolism treatment note.
- Added the official Steer source to the Case File programme statement and unified the programme description. Public-site alignment is verified; exact brand approval is pending.
- Retained glucose-dependent insulin wording, natural hormone versus medicine distinction, medicine-specific safety limitations, lean-mass/function distinction, withdrawal-study limitations and US-versus-India regulatory caveats. No dosing, eligibility score, diagnosis or outcome promise was introduced.
